Insights & recommendations

Findings from the full 2013–2014 sample

  1. 01

    More units sold, less revenue

    Revenue fell 17.6% in 2014, even as units sold rose 9.9%.

    I’d start with Government, where revenue fell $36.4M, and compare units and revenue per unit for Amarilla and VTT.

  2. 02

    Two products accounted for 75% of gross profit

    VTT and Amarilla led on profit, but VTT revenue fell $25.6M in 2014.

    I’d review VTT in the United States and Canada first. They accounted for about $15M of its decline.

  3. 03

    The 7% band recorded the most gross profit

    The 10% band sold the most units, but gross profit was $58.15M versus $75.34M at 7%.

    I’d test both rates on the same product in the same market and compare total gross profit.

Source: Financial Sample workbook · 1,064 sales records

Metric definitions

Net revenue is gross sales less discounts. Gross profit subtracts cost of goods sold (COGS) from net revenue. Gross margin is total gross profit divided by total net revenue. The discount rate is total discounts divided by gross sales.
